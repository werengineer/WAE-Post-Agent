# Blog research brief (WAE) — drafted 2026-11-12

## Positioning (from public listings; confirm with team)
We Are Engineer (WAE): engineering ecosystem/community (launched 2023) — interactive features, AI integration, community. Gap it addresses: formal education vs. industry needs. Audience: engineering students and early-career engineers in India. Not verified against weareengineer.com (not reachable via search).

## Sources & figures used (secondary sources; verify before publishing)
- India Skills Report 2026 (Wheebox) via Careers360: BE/BTech employability ~70.15% (vs 71.5% in 2025); CS 80%, IT 78%, Instrumentation 77%, ECE 75%, Mechanical 63%. https://news.careers360.com/india-skills-report-2026-cs-computer-science-it-engineer-top-jobs-mba-employment-decline-ai-ml-bfsi-fmcg-pharma-women-hiring
- CodePath survey of 200+ engineering leaders (Fortune, Dec 2025): entry-level tech hiring slow; portfolios and internships seen as top readiness signals. Only the summary was read. https://fortune.com/2025/12/18/tech-hiring-slow-but-not-fully-stalled-exclusive-codepath-data-new-secret-to-land-tech-role/
- NACE (via ASEE paper): 70%+ of employers say internship experience is critical. https://peer.asee.org/impact-of-engineering-students-internship-experiences-on-career-development.pdf
- The Interview Guys (1,500 grads; 81.6% vs 40.7% hired with/without internships; NACE Job Outlook 2026 +1.6%). Vendor blog — NOT used in drafts as a hard stat.
