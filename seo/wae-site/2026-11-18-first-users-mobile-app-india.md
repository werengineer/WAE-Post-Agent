# How to Get Your First Users for a Mobile App (Without Fake Installs)

## The short answer

Get your first users from people you can reach directly. Talk to them, watch how they use the app, fix what confuses them, and only then spend money on ads. Count real, active users. Do not count installs alone. Never buy bot installs.

## Step 1: Know who the app is for

Write one sentence: "This app helps [type of person] do [one task]." If you cannot write it, your store page will not be clear either. Pick one group first. A group you can find in specific places (a college club, a WhatsApp group, a forum, a subreddit) is easier to reach than "everyone".

## Step 2: Get your first 10 to 50 users by hand

- Message people in the group from Step 1 and ask them to try the app.
- Ask each person to use it while you watch or listen. Write down where they get stuck.
- Ask for one sentence of feedback. Fix the top problem before you invite more people.
- Post in communities where the topic is allowed. Read the rules first. Say you built the app.

Early users who tell you what is wrong are worth more than a large number of quiet downloads.

## Step 3: Fix your store listing

Your Play Store or App Store page is where most people decide. Check:

- The title says what the app does in plain words.
- The first two lines of the description answer "what is it for?".
- Screenshots show the main task, with short captions.
- Ratings come from real users. Ask happy users to rate, after they have used the app for a while.

## Step 4: Measure retention, not only installs

An install is a download. A retained user comes back. Track, at least:

- Installs.
- How many users finish the first key action (for example, sign up or the first task).
- How many return after day 1 and after day 7.

If many people install and few return, more installs will not fix the problem. The app or the onboarding needs work.

## Step 5: Add referrals, then paid ads

When users come back on their own, add a simple referral prompt. Use paid ads after you know which message and which audience work. Ads on a leaky app waste money.

## Why bot installs hurt you

Bot or fake installs are download numbers that do not come from real people. They:

- Break store policies and can put your app or developer account at risk.
- Give you false data. You cannot tell what works.
- Bring no feedback, no ratings, and no revenue.

Install numbers that come from verified accounts are more useful than larger numbers that nobody can check.

## FAQ

### How do I get my first 100 users for a mobile app?
Start with people you can reach directly, such as a community or group your app is made for. Ask them to try it, watch where they get stuck, and fix those problems. Invite more people only after the main problems are fixed.

### Is it okay to buy installs for my app?
No. Bought or bot installs break store policies and can put your app or account at risk. They also give you false data and no real feedback. Real users from a defined group are safer and more useful.

### What matters more, installs or retention?
Retention. An install shows someone downloaded the app. Retention shows they came back. If few users return after day 1 and day 7, fix the app or onboarding before getting more installs.

### When should I start paid ads?
After real users come back on their own and you know which message and audience work. Ads sent to an app that users leave quickly will waste your budget.
