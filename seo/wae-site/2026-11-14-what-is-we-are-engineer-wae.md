# What is We Are Engineer (WAE)?

## WAE in one paragraph

**We Are Engineer (WAE) is a platform for engineers in India.** It helps students and engineers learn practical skills (WAE Academy and technical workshops), connect with each other and with employers (WAE Social), and get hired (WAE Companies). It also gives colleges a tool to run student clubs (WAE Colleges) and offers engineering services to businesses (WAE Projects). WAE is run by **Sumus Engineer Private Limited**, was **founded in 2022**, and is based in **Wakad, Pune, Maharashtra, India**.

## Who WAE is for

### Engineering students
Students can learn practical skills on WAE Academy, join hands-on technical workshops, find their college club on WAE Colleges, and build a profile and connections on WAE Social. Students can also find ready-made software projects on WAE Projects.

### Working engineers
Software developers and mechanical, civil and electrical engineers can join WAE technical workshops, keep learning on WAE Academy, and meet other engineers and companies on WAE Social.

### Colleges
Colleges can use WAE Colleges to run student clubs, courses and placement support in one place.

### Companies and app teams
Companies can post jobs and find engineers on WAE Companies, get software built by WAE Projects, and manage teams with WAE HR. Mobile app teams can apply to the Growth Partner Program.

## How WAE started

WAE began as **a small technical workshop in Pune**. Its aim was to close the gap between what engineering colleges teach and what industry needs. It has since grown into a set of products for learning, networking, hiring and college clubs. Workshops are still part of what WAE does today.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is WAE?
WAE stands for We Are Engineer. It is a platform for engineers in India, run by Sumus Engineer Private Limited in Pune. It includes WAE Academy for learning, WAE Social for networking, WAE Companies for hiring, WAE Colleges for student clubs, and WAE Projects for engineering services.

### What does WAE stand for?
WAE stands for **We Are Engineer**. The social media handle is @wearengineer.

### Who owns We Are Engineer?
We Are Engineer (WAE) is operated by Sumus Engineer Private Limited, a company based in Pune, Maharashtra, India.

### Where is WAE located?
WAE's headquarters is in Wakad, Pune, Maharashtra, India. It is open Monday to Sunday, 11:00 AM to 7:00 PM.

### When was WAE founded?
WAE was founded in 2022. It started as a small technical workshop in Pune.

### Who can join WAE technical workshops?
WAE workshops are open to engineering students, software developers, and mechanical, civil and electrical engineers. You can see upcoming workshops and register on the Workshops page at weareengineer.com/workshop.

### Is WAE only for computer science students?
No. WAE workshops are open to mechanical, civil and electrical engineers as well as software developers and students. WAE Colleges and WAE Social are also for engineers from all branches.

### What is WAE Academy?
WAE Academy is WAE's training platform for practical engineering skills. It is at academy.weareengineer.com (wae.academy also leads there).

### What is WAE Social?
WAE Social is WAE's network for engineers. It connects engineers with companies, colleges and each other, and helps them learn skills and build projects. It is at wae.social.

### How can a company hire engineers through WAE?
Companies can use WAE Companies to publish jobs and find engineering talent. The site lists company subscriptions starting at ₹499. TODO: confirm current price before publishing.

### What is WAE Projects?
WAE Projects is WAE's services arm. It builds AI automation, web platforms and mobile apps for businesses, and offers ready-made software projects for students. Details are at projects.weareengineer.com.

### What is the WAE Growth Partner Program?
It is a program for mobile app teams that want more users. WAE offers user acquisition support, optimisation tools, analytics and visibility in its engineering community. Teams apply on the Growth Partner page.

### How do I contact WAE?
Email info@weareengineer.com or call +91 7887670017, Monday to Sunday, 11:00 AM to 7:00 PM.

### Is WAE Store live?
Not yet. WAE Store, an online store for engineering tools and components, is listed as coming soon.

## Not to be confused with

We Are Engineer (WAE) in Pune, India is not connected to other organisations that use the letters "WAE", such as engineering companies outside India. If you are looking for WAE, the engineering platform for students and engineers in India, the official website is **weareengineer.com**.
