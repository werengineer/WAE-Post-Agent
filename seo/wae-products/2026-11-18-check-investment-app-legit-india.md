# How to Check if an Investment App Is Legit in India

Fake investment apps and websites are common. You can catch most of them with five checks that take about ten minutes. This guide lists those checks in order, and says where to report a fraud.

*Checks below follow public guidance from SEBI-linked and news sources listed under "Sources". Rules change, so confirm on the official sites.*

## Quick answer

An investment app is more likely to be genuine if: the company is registered with SEBI for what it offers, the app shows the Play Store verified badge where available, the developer name matches the official website, you pay only into the firm's own business account, and it does not promise fast, high returns.

## 1. Check SEBI registration

Use SEBI's official intermediary search to see whether a broker, investment adviser or other intermediary is registered. Match the name, registration number and contact details with what the app and website show. If they do not match, stop.

## 2. Look for the verified badge

Google has started labelling verified investment apps on the Play Store, working with SEBI (Business Today, March 2026). Treat the badge as one signal, not proof. Still cross-check the registration on SEBI's site.

## 3. Match the developer and the download source

- Download only from the official app store listing linked from the company's own website.
- Do not install apps sent through WhatsApp, Telegram or social media messages.
- The developer name and contact details in the store should match the company website.

## 4. Check where the money goes

Be careful if you are asked to pay into a personal account, or to a person instead of a company. Be careful too if you are added to a random tips group. These are listed as warning signs in the guidance below.

## 5. Distrust fast, high returns

If an app promises great returns in a short time, treat it as a red flag.

## What to do if you think it is a scam

- Report online financial fraud on the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal, or call 1930.
- For complaints about SEBI-regulated entities, use SEBI's SCORES.
- Tell your bank straight away if you already sent money.

## FAQ

### How do I check if an investment app is registered in India?
Search the firm on SEBI's official intermediary search. Match the name, registration number and contact details with the app and its website. If any detail differs, do not invest.

### What is the Play Store verified badge for investment apps?
Google has begun labelling verified investment apps on the Play Store, in collaboration with SEBI. Look for the badge, then still confirm the registration on SEBI's site.

### What are the red flags of a fake investment app?
Promises of fast, high returns, requests to pay into a personal account, and links that arrive from unknown WhatsApp, Telegram or social media groups.

### Where do I report a fake investment app in India?
Use the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal or call 1930. For SEBI-regulated entities, use SCORES.

### Is checking an app enough to know it is safe?
No. Checks reduce risk but do not remove it. Read the terms and fees and never invest money you cannot afford to lose. This guide is general information, not financial advice.
