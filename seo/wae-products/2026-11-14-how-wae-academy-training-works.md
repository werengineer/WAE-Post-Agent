## The short answer

WAE Academy teaches software development in four phases: hands-on projects, mentorship from working engineers, team sprints, and career support. Its stated aim is to close the gap between what colleges teach and what the tech industry needs.

## Phase 1: Hands-on projects

The curriculum is built around real-world projects. WAE Academy says about 80% of the learning is practical work. You build things instead of only watching lessons.

## Phase 2: Mentorship from working engineers

Mentors are senior software engineers. They give 1-on-1 code reviews, so you get feedback on your own code. TODO: number of mentors and the mentor-to-student ratio.

## Phase 3: Team sprints

Students work in squads. Each squad uses Git and sprint planning, the way many software teams work. This practises teamwork, not only coding.

## Phase 4: Placement and career support

Career support includes resume help, mock interviews and hiring coordination. This is support, not a promise of a job. For the written terms of any placement or internship promise, see the Placements page. TODO: link the written terms.

WAE Academy was founded in 2022. Its about page names Lokesh Kabra (Founder & CEO), Deepanshu Sharma (COO) and Vishal Lama (VP). It is part of We Are Engineer (WAE), a platform for engineers in India.
