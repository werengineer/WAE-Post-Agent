# How long do press-on nails last?

## The short answer

A press-on set can last anywhere from a weekend to about two weeks. Prep, glue or tabs, how firmly you press, and what you do with your hands decide where you land. Nail glue lasts longer than adhesive tabs. Tabs are the better pick for a short wear.

## What decides how long a set lasts

Three things matter most:

1. **Prep.** Clean, dry, lightly buffed nails hold best. Oil, lotion and old polish stop the adhesive from gripping.
2. **Adhesive.** Nail glue gives a longer hold. Adhesive tabs give a shorter, gentler one.
3. **Pressure and habits.** Firm, even pressure on each nail when you apply it matters. So does how much water, soap and heavy hand work the nails see afterwards.

## How to get the longest wear

1. Wash and dry your hands. Push back cuticles gently. Do not cut them.
2. Lightly buff the nail surface, then wipe each nail with the alcohol prep pad.
3. Pick the right size for each finger. A nail that fits edge to edge sits flatter and lifts less. See the [size guide](/size-guide).
4. Apply adhesive, place the nail from the cuticle toward the tip, and press firmly on the centre and sides.
5. Hold each nail for about 20 to 30 seconds. (Brands and beauty editors give hold times in this range. TODO: confirm the time the Velvet Tips team recommends.)
6. Go easy on water, soaking and heavy hand work for the first hours. Wear gloves for cleaning.

More detail: [how to apply press-on nails](/how-to-apply).

## What shortens wear time

- Skipping prep, or applying over oil or lotion
- A size that is too big or too small
- Long soaks, hot water and cleaning chemicals
- Using nails as tools to open cans or scrape labels
- Very long shapes that catch on clothes and bags (shorter shapes snag less)

## Signs it is time to take them off

A nail that lifts at the edge, traps water underneath, or a set you are tired of. Do not peel or pull. Soak in warm soapy water and lift from the sides. Pulling is what harms natural nails. ## FAQ

### How long do press-on nails last?
From a weekend to about two weeks, depending on prep, adhesive and how you use your hands. Nail glue lasts longer than adhesive tabs.

### Do press-on nails last longer with glue or tabs?
Glue. Tabs are meant for shorter wear and are easier to take off.

### Can I make press-on nails last longer?
Yes. Start with clean, dry, lightly buffed nails, choose the right size, press firmly, and limit soaking and heavy hand work.

### Can I reuse press-on nails after wearing them?
Yes, if you remove them gently by soaking in warm soapy water and lifting from the sides, then store them in their case.

### Why do my press-on nails keep popping off?
Usually oil or lotion on the nail, a size that does not fit, too little pressure when applying, or a lot of water exposure. Check these first.

### Can I shower or wash dishes with press-on nails?
Short contact with water is fine once they are set. Long soaks and hot water loosen the hold. Wear gloves for dishes.
