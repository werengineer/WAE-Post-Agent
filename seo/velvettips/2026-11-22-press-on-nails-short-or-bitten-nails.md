# Can you wear press-on nails if your nails are short or bitten?

## The short answer

Yes. Press-on nails cover the whole natural nail, so they work on short nails and on nails that have been bitten. The one thing to know is that a small nail gives the press-on less surface to hold on to. So short nails need careful sizing, good prep and the right adhesive. Done well, a set can last a good while and can also help you leave your nails alone.

## Why short nails are harder

A press-on stays on because it sticks to your natural nail. The smaller the nail, the smaller the area that holds it. Doonails, another press-on brand, says the same in its support article: with very small or heavily bitten nails, press-ons may be less resistant and wear out sooner, whether you use tabs or glue.

That is not a reason to skip them. It is a reason to take a little more care at each step below.

## Pick a shape that suits short nails

Long, pointed shapes need some length to look right. On short nails, rounder or softer shapes usually look more natural and feel easier to wear.

Velvet Tips sells four shapes: almond, coffin, oval and squoval. For short nails, oval and squoval are the softer options. Squoval is a square shape with rounded corners, so there is no sharp corner to catch on things.

You can also file any set shorter and rounder. Do this before you apply it, not after.

## Get the size right

Size matters more on a small nail. A press-on that is too wide tends to lift at the sides.

The Velvet Tips size guide gives these steps:

1. Wash and dry your hands and push your cuticles back so you can see the whole nail.
2. Lay a strip of flexible tape or paper across the widest part of each nail, then measure it against a ruler.
3. Measure all ten nails. Your thumbs and your two hands often differ.
4. Pick the closest listed size for each nail.
5. If you fall between two sizes, choose the smaller one and file the edges to fit.

Not sure? You can send your measurements on WhatsApp and the team will help.

## Prep: the five minutes that matter most

The Velvet Tips how-to-apply guide gives this order:

1. Push your cuticles back.
2. Buff the shine off each nail.
3. Wipe each nail with the alcohol pad to remove oil and lotion.
4. Hold each press-on against its nail before you glue it. It should cover the nail from side to side without touching the skin.
5. Press the tip on at a slight angle at the cuticle, lower it flat and hold for 20 to 30 seconds. Start with your thumb.

Every set comes with a kit: a nail file or buffer, adhesive tabs, nail glue, a cuticle pusher and an alcohol prep pad.

## Glue or tabs?

Velvet Tips says to use adhesive tabs for shorter wear and nail glue for longer wear. On short nails, where the grip area is small, glue is the safer choice if you want the set to last. Use tabs when you want a set for one evening.

## If you bite your nails

Press-ons can be a useful break from biting. The nail is covered, and a polished set gives you a reason to leave it alone. A few things help:

- **Check the skin first.** Do not apply a set over broken or sore skin around the nail. Let it settle first. TODO: have a nail professional review this line.
- **Do not bite the press-ons.** Biting can lift the edge and pull at your natural nail.
- **Expect some variation.** If your nails are very small, the set may wear out sooner than it would on longer nails.
- **Rest your nails between sets.** The Velvet Tips FAQ says damage usually comes from pulling a set off, and that you should rest your nails between sets.

## Taking them off without hurting your nails

Never pull a set straight off. From the Velvet Tips guide:

1. Soak your hands in warm, soapy water for 10 to 15 minutes.
2. Lift each press-on from the side with a cuticle stick.
3. Remove any leftover adhesive with an alcohol pad.

If you remove a set gently and keep it in its case, you can wear it again.

## FAQ

### Can you wear press-on nails if you have very short nails?
Yes. Press-ons cover the whole natural nail, so they suit short nails. Pick the right size for each nail, prep well and use nail glue for longer wear. Very small nails give less grip, so a set may wear out sooner than on longer nails.

### Can you wear press-on nails if you bite your nails?
Yes, if the skin around the nail is healthy. Press-ons cover the nail and can give you a reason to stop biting. Avoid biting the press-ons themselves, because that lifts the edges.

### What is the best press-on shape for short nails?
Softer, rounder shapes are usually easiest on short nails. At Velvet Tips, oval and squoval are the rounder options. You can also file any set shorter and rounder before you apply it.

### What if my size falls between two sizes?
Choose the smaller size and file the edges to fit. A press-on that is too wide tends to lift at the sides.

### Should I use glue or tabs on short nails?
Use nail glue if you want longer wear, and adhesive tabs for shorter wear. Both come in every Velvet Tips kit.

### Will press-ons damage short or bitten nails?
Not when you apply and remove them carefully. Damage usually comes from pulling a set off. Soak, lift from the side and rest your nails between sets.

### Can I wear the same set again?
Yes, if you remove it gently and store it in its case.
