# Nail glue vs adhesive tabs: which should you use for press-on nails?

**Published:** 2026-11-15 · **By:** Velvet Tips <!-- TODO: add a named author (for example a founder) if the team agrees. This helps E-E-A-T. -->

## The short answer

Use **adhesive tabs** when you want to wear a set for a short time and take it off easily. Use **nail glue** when you want the set to stay on longer. Nail glue holds longer than tabs. Every Velvet Tips set comes with both, so you can choose each time you wear it.

## Glue vs tabs at a glance

| | Adhesive tabs | Nail glue |
|---|---|---|
| Best for | Short wear: an event, a weekend, trying a new shape | Longer wear |
| Hold | Lighter | Stronger, lasts longer |
| Removal | Easier. Soak and lift. | Needs a longer soak in warm soapy water. Then lift from the side. |
| Clean-up before reuse | Peel off what is left of the tab | Clean off leftover glue before you store the set |
| In the Velvet Tips kit? | Yes | Yes |

## When to choose adhesive tabs

Pick tabs if:

- You are wearing the set for a short time, such as a wedding, a party, a shoot or a weekend.
- You want to switch designs often.
- You are new to press-ons and want to practise placement and sizing first.
- You want the simplest removal.

Tabs are sticky pads that sit between your natural nail and the press-on. They give a lighter hold than glue, so expect them to suit short wear rather than long wear.

## When to choose nail glue

Pick glue if:

- You want the set to stay on as long as possible.
- You use your hands a lot during the day.
- You have worn a set with tabs and it lifted sooner than you wanted.

Velvet Tips' advice for glue is to use a **thin layer on the press-on and a small amount on your nail**. More glue does not mean a better hold. A thick layer can squeeze out onto your skin and makes removal harder.

## How to apply press-ons with adhesive tabs

1. **Prep.** Push back your cuticles gently, buff the shine off each nail and wipe it with the alcohol prep pad. No oil or lotion should be left.
2. **Lay out your sizes.** Match one press-on to each nail. If you are between two sizes, choose the smaller one and file the edges.
3. **Add a tab.** Peel a tab and press it onto your natural nail. Smooth it down so there are no air bubbles. Peel off the top film.
4. **Press and hold.** Set the press-on at a slight angle near the cuticle, lower it flat and press firmly for 20 to 30 seconds.

## How to apply press-ons with nail glue

1. **Prep** exactly as above. Clean, dry, buffed nails hold glue best.
2. **Lay out your sizes** before you open the glue.
3. **Glue both surfaces.** Put a thin layer of glue on the back of the press-on and a small amount on your natural nail.
4. **Press and hold.** Start near the cuticle at a slight angle, lower it flat and hold firmly for 20 to 30 seconds. Many people start with the thumb.
5. **Keep it dry at first.** Avoid long soaks and hot water for the first hour. Water-softened nails do not hold glue as well.

## Removing each one so you can wear the set again

Do not pull a set straight off with either option. Damage to natural nails usually comes from pulling, not from wearing.

1. **Soak** your hands in warm soapy water to loosen the tab or glue. Glue needs a longer soak.
2. **Lift from the side** gently, not from the tip. If it resists, soak a little longer.
3. **Clean and store.** Remove leftover tab or glue from the back of each press-on and put the set back in its case.
4. **Rest your nails.** Give them a day or two without anything on before your next set.

## Why press-ons lift (with either option)

If a set lifts early, the cause is usually one of these:

- **Oil on the nail.** Lotion, hand cream or natural oils weaken the bond. Wipe with the alcohol pad just before you apply.
- **Shine not buffed off.** A smooth, shiny nail gives the adhesive less to grip.
- **Wrong size.** A press-on that is too wide touches the skin at the sides and lifts. Check our size guide.
- **Not enough pressure.** Press and hold each nail for the full 20 to 30 seconds.
- **Water too soon.** Avoid long soaks in the first hour.
- **Tabs used for long wear.** If you need more time, switch to glue.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which lasts longer, nail glue or adhesive tabs?
Nail glue lasts longer than adhesive tabs. How long either lasts depends on how well you prep your nails and how much you use your hands. Clean, dry, buffed nails and firm pressure give the longest wear.

### Are adhesive tabs better for your natural nails?
Tabs are easier to remove, which makes it easier to avoid pulling. With either option, damage usually comes from pulling a set off. Soak, lift gently from the side, and rest your nails between sets.

### Can I reuse press-on nails after using glue?
Yes. Soak your hands in warm soapy water, lift the set gently from the sides, clean off the leftover glue and store the set in its case.

### Do Velvet Tips sets come with glue and tabs?
Yes. Every set comes with a kit: a nail file or buffer, adhesive tabs, nail glue, a cuticle pusher and an alcohol prep pad.

### Can I use tabs one time and glue the next time with the same set?
Yes. Clean the back of each press-on fully before you apply it again, so no old tab or glue is left.

### How long should I press each nail?
Hold each press-on firmly for 20 to 30 seconds.

### Why do my press-ons pop off on the first day?
The usual causes are oil left on the nail, shine not buffed off, a size that is too wide, too little pressure, or water in the first hour. Fix these first. If you used tabs and need longer wear, switch to glue.
