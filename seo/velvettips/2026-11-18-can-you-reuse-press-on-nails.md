# Can you reuse press-on nails? Yes, if you take them off gently

**Published:** 2026-11-18 · **By:** Velvet Tips <!-- TODO: add a named author (for example a founder) if the team agrees. -->

## The short answer

Yes. Velvet Tips sets are reusable. You can wear a set again if you remove it gently: soak it, lift each nail from the side and never pull it straight off. Then clean off the old adhesive and keep the set in its case. How many times a set lasts depends on how you apply it, how you remove it and how you look after it.

## What decides whether a set can be worn again

- **How you take it off.** Pulling a nail off bends it and can crack it. Soaking and lifting from the side keeps the shape.
- **Which adhesive you used.** Adhesive tabs suit short wear and come off more easily. Nail glue lasts longer but takes more care to remove and clean.
- **How well it was applied.** Clean, dry, buffed nails and firm pressure for 20 to 30 seconds give a good hold and make gentle removal easier.
- **How you store it.** A clean nail kept in its case stays in shape and does not get lost or scratched.

## How to remove a set so it can be reused

1. **Soak.** Soak your hands in warm, soapy water for 10 to 15 minutes to loosen the adhesive.
2. **Lift from the side.** Use a cuticle stick to lift each nail gently from one side. Never pull it straight off.
3. **Go slowly.** If a nail does not lift, soak for a few more minutes. Do not force it.

## How to clean each nail

1. Take off the old adhesive from the underside of the nail while it is still soft from the soak.
2. Wipe the nail with an alcohol pad to remove leftover residue.
3. Let it dry fully before you put it away.
4. Clean your own nails too. Remove leftover adhesive before the next application so the next set sits well.

## How to store a set between wears

- Keep the set in its case.
- Make sure every nail is clean and dry first.
- Keep nails together by size so you can find the right one quickly next time.

## Can you reuse a set applied with nail glue?

It can be done. Nail glue holds longer, so it takes more patience to remove and clean. Soak for the full 10 to 15 minutes, lift from the side and clean the underside well. If you plan to wear the same set several times, adhesive tabs are the easier choice for short wear. Every Velvet Tips kit includes both tabs and nail glue, so you can pick each time. Read more in our guide to nail glue and adhesive tabs. <!-- TODO: link to /blog/nail-glue-vs-adhesive-tabs once it is published (draft exists on branch bot/seo-velvettips-2026-11-15). -->

## When to stop reusing a nail

Retire a nail, and wear the rest of the set or choose a new set, if it:

- is cracked or chipped,
- has lost its curve or no longer sits flat on your nail,
- still has adhesive that will not come off,
- does not hold when you press it on.

## Give your own nails a rest

Reusing a set is easy on your wallet, but your natural nails still need care. Leave them bare for a day or two between sets, moisturise your cuticles with a nourishing oil and file in one direction. If your nails feel thin or sore after removal, pause for a few days. See our guide: [Nail care between sets](/blog/nail-care-between-sets).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I wear a press-on set more than once?
Yes. Remove the set gently by soaking and lifting from the side, clean off the adhesive and store it in its case. Then you can wear it again.

### How many times can I reuse press-on nails?
It depends on how the set is applied, removed and stored. Velvet Tips has not published a fixed number. If you are unsure whether a nail is still good, check that it is not cracked and that it still sits flat on your nail. Ask the team on WhatsApp if you want advice. <!-- TODO: add a confirmed number of wears if the team provides one. -->

### How do I get the old glue off press-on nails?
Soak the set in warm, soapy water so the adhesive softens, then remove the residue from the underside and wipe with an alcohol pad. Let the nails dry fully.

### Should I use adhesive tabs or nail glue if I want to reuse a set?
Adhesive tabs suit short wear and are easier to remove. Nail glue lasts longer. Both can work, but removal is gentler with tabs.

### How should I store press-on nails?
Clean and dry, in their case, and together by size.

### Do press-on nails damage my natural nails when I reuse them?
Not when they are applied and removed carefully. Damage usually comes from pulling a set off. Soak and lift, and rest your nails between sets.

### Can I reuse custom-design press-ons?
Yes, the same care applies. Remove gently, clean, and store in the case. <!-- TODO: confirm with the team whether custom sets are made the same way and reusable in the same way. -->
