# Are press-on nails cheaper than salon nails?

## The short answer

It depends on how often you wear them and how many times you reuse a set. A press-on set costs more on the day than a basic salon service, but you can wear a well-cared-for set more than once. A salon service is done once. So the honest way to compare is cost per wear, not the price tag. Below, we use the real prices on velvettips.com to show how.

## How to work out cost per wear

Use this simple sum:

**Cost per wear = price of the set ÷ number of times you wear it**

Example using a ₹999 set (the number of wears is yours to choose, this is only arithmetic):

- Worn once: ₹999 per wear.
- Worn twice: ₹499.50 per wear.
- Worn three times: ₹333 per wear.

Compare that with a service price you pay each time, for example ₹299 for Basic Art or ₹799 for Gel Extension. A set you wear once costs more than a ₹299 service. A set you wear several times can cost less per wear than a ₹799 service. Where you land depends on your habits.

## What the price does not show

- **Time.** Our guide on press-ons vs salon nails says a salon appointment can take one to two hours, while a press-on set takes about 15 to 20 minutes at home once you know the routine.
- **Travel.** A set is applied at home. A service means going to the studio.
- **Design.** Press-ons can carry detailed artwork that would be slow to hand-paint. You can also send your own design. See /custom.
- **Wear time.** Salon gel usually lasts longer. Press-ons generally last several days to a couple of weeks, depending on prep and lifestyle.
- **Care.** A set only stays reusable if you apply and remove it gently. See /how-to-apply.
