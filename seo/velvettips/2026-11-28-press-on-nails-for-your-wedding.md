# Can you wear press-on nails on your wedding day?

Yes, you can. A well-fitted set, applied with care, is a practical choice for a bride: you can choose the design weeks ahead, try it at home and put it on in minutes. This guide covers how to choose, test and apply a set, and what to keep in your bag on the day.

## The short answer

Press-ons can work for a wedding day if three things are right: the size fits each nail, the nails are prepared properly, and you have tried the set once before. For a longer event, use nail glue rather than tabs. Velvet Tips' own guide says tabs suit short wear and glue suits longer wear. If you have a multi-day wedding, plan separate looks for each function.

## Press-ons or a salon set for the bride?

Both are valid. Here is how they differ in plain terms.

- **Press-ons:** you choose and own the set in advance. You can test it, and you can wear it again if you remove it gently (Velvet Tips sets are reusable). You apply it yourself, or someone helps you.
- **Salon or studio nails:** a nail artist does the work for you, so the fit and finish are handled in person. Velvet Tips also offers studio services in Pune, including a Bridal Package at ₹2,999 (see below).

If you are nervous about doing your own application, book a studio session or ask a friend to help. If you like to plan ahead and control the design, press-ons suit you. For the cost comparison, see our guide on press-ons versus salon nails.

## Pick the set: shape, colour and finish

- **Shape:** choose the shape you are comfortable doing daily tasks in. Rounder, shorter shapes feel easier to handle on a busy day. Almond and oval are popular for bridal looks. (Bridal Pearl is oval; Ivory Gold Bridal is almond.)
- **Match your outfit and jewellery.** One bridal-nail guide advises letting jewellery and outfit colour guide the choice. Pearl, gold, ivory and blush work with many outfits.
- **Several functions, several looks.** Some brides plan a different set for each event, for example a softer look for the ceremony and bolder nails for the reception. Press-ons make this simple, because each set is ready before the week begins.

## Do a trial run before the wedding

Do not wear a set for the first time on the wedding day.

1. Order early. TODO: confirm delivery times for your city; the site says delivery times depend on your location and are confirmed with your order.
2. Measure each nail at its widest point and match it to the size guide. If you are between sizes, choose the smaller one and file the edges. If unsure, message Velvet Tips on WhatsApp.
3. Apply the set at home some days before, using the same method you plan to use on the day.
4. Notice how it feels, whether any nail lifts, and how long you are comfortable with it.
5. Remove it gently: soak in warm soapy water for 10 to 15 minutes and lift from the side. Never pull it straight off.

## How to put them on for the day

These are the steps on the Velvet Tips how-to-apply page.

1. **Prep.** Push back your cuticles gently, buff the shine off each nail and wipe each nail with an alcohol pad to remove oil or lotion.
2. **Check sizes.** Hold each press-on against its nail. It should cover nail edge to nail edge without touching skin.
3. **Add adhesive.** For a long event, use a thin layer of nail glue on the press-on and a small amount on your nail.
4. **Press.** Set the press-on at a slight angle at the cuticle, lower it flat and hold firmly for 20 to 30 seconds. Start with the thumb and work outward.

## What to carry on the day

Each Velvet Tips set comes with a kit: a nail file or buffer, adhesive tabs, nail glue, a cuticle pusher and an alcohol prep pad. Keep the kit, or part of it, in your bag or with a trusted person:

- the nail glue and tabs, in case one nail lifts
- the file or buffer, for a snag
- the cuticle pusher and alcohol pad, if you need to re-fix a nail

Be gentle with water and force during the day. Opening cans or packets with your nails and long hand-washing can loosen any press-on.
