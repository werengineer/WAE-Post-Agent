# Custom press-on nails: how to turn your own design into a set

## The short answer

Custom press-on nails are a set made from artwork you choose. At Velvet Tips you upload a photo you love or your own illustration on the [Custom designs page](https://velvettips.com/custom). The team checks that the design prints well, then confirms the price and timeline with you on WhatsApp. Only after that is your design printed on a set in your size and shape, and shipped to you.

## What counts as a good design

Velvet Tips accepts "a photo you love or your own illustration." Clear, high-resolution artwork prints best. Some ideas:

- A pattern or illustration you drew yourself.
- A photo of a print, fabric, painting or artwork you love.
- A colour palette taken from an outfit for a special day.

If you are not sure whether your idea will work, send it anyway. Reviewing each design is part of the process.

## Artwork file rules

The upload form asks for:

- **Format:** PNG, JPG or WebP.
- **Size:** up to 4 MB.
- **Quality:** clear, high-resolution artwork. A blurry screenshot will not print as well as the original file.

## Choose your size and shape

The order form needs your size: XS, S, M or L. The shape is optional. You can choose Almond, Coffin, Oval, Square, Squoval or Stiletto.

- To find your size, measure the widest part of each natural nail and match it to the [size guide](https://velvettips.com/size-guide).
- If you are between sizes, the FAQ suggests choosing the smaller one and filing the edges.
- If you are still unsure, message the team on WhatsApp.

## The three steps, from upload to your door

1. **Upload.** Send your artwork on the Custom designs page, with your name, WhatsApp number, size and design. Email, shape and notes are optional.
2. **Review.** The team checks that the design prints well and confirms the price and timeline with you on WhatsApp.
3. **Print.** Your design is printed on a press-on set in your chosen size and shape, then shipped to you.

## Can you wear and reuse a custom set?

Velvet Tips sets are reusable. Remove a set gently by soaking your hands and lifting from the sides, then store it in its case. Adhesive tabs suit short wear and nail glue suits longer wear. ## Mistakes that make a design print badly

- Uploading a low-resolution or blurry image.
- Sending a file over 4 MB or in a format other than PNG, JPG or WebP.
- Guessing your size. Measure the widest part of each nail, or ask on WhatsApp.
- Leaving out notes. If a detail matters to you, write it in the notes field.
