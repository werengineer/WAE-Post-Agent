# How to find your press-on nail size

**Published:** 2026-11-14 · **By:** Velvet Tips <!-- TODO: add a named author (for example a founder) if the team agrees; helps E-E-A-T. -->

## The short answer

Measure the widest part of each natural nail, then pick the press-on size that is closest to that width. If a nail falls between two sizes, choose the smaller one and file the edges to fit. A press-on that is too wide lifts at the sides. Measure all ten nails, because your thumbs and your two hands often differ.

## What you need

- Flexible tape or a thin strip of paper
- A ruler
- A pen and paper to note each nail
- Clean, dry hands

## Step 1: Prepare your nails

Wash and dry your hands. Push your cuticles back gently so the whole nail is visible. If the cuticle covers part of the nail, you will measure it narrower than it really is.

## Step 2: Measure the widest point of each nail

Lay the tape or paper strip straight across the widest part of the nail, side to side. Mark the edges with your fingertip or a pen, then hold the strip against the ruler. Write down the number for each of the ten nails. Do not copy one hand to the other: measure both.

## Step 3: Match each nail to a size

Open the product page of the set you want and compare your measurements with the sizes shown there. Choose the size that is closest for each nail. Every Velvet Tips set comes in four sizes, so you can usually cover all ten nails from one set.

## Step 4: What to do if you are between two sizes

Choose the smaller size and file the edges until it sits comfortably within your side walls. A press-on that is too wide tends to lift at the sides and catch on things. If you are still unsure, message the team on WhatsApp and they will help you choose.

## How to check the fit before you glue

Before adding adhesive, hold each press-on against its nail. It should cover the nail from side to side without sitting on the skin around it, and you should not see a gap of bare nail at the edges. Lay out all ten press-ons in order, then match them to your fingers. This is the "lay out your sizes" step in our [how-to-apply guide](/how-to-apply).

## Signs your press-on is the wrong size

- **Lifts at the sides:** it is probably too wide. Go down a size or file the edges.
- **Bare nail shows at the edges:** it is probably too narrow. Go up a size.
- **Presses on the skin around the nail:** it is too wide for that finger.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I find my press-on nail size?
Measure the widest part of each natural nail with flexible tape or a paper strip, read it against a ruler, and match it to the closest size on the product page.

### Do I need to measure all ten nails?
Yes. Thumbs are usually wider than other fingers, and your left and right hands can differ.

### What if I am between two sizes?
Choose the smaller size and file the edges to fit. A press-on that is too wide lifts at the sides.

### Are press-on nail sizes the same across brands?
Not always. Brands use their own size charts, so check the chart for the brand you are buying from rather than relying on a size you used before.

### Can I file a press-on that is slightly too wide?
Yes. Filing the edges is the fix we recommend when you are between two sizes. Pick the smaller size and file it to fit. Do not go smaller if bare nail would show at the sides.

### Who can help me choose a size?
Message the Velvet Tips team on WhatsApp if you are unsure.
