# Press-on nails: glue or tabs, and how long will they last?

## The short answer

Use **adhesive tabs** when you want a short, easy wear, for example a party or a weekend. Use **nail glue** when you want the set to stay on longer. Glue holds longer than tabs. Tabs are gentler and easier to take off. Every Velvet Tips set comes with both, so you can choose for each occasion.

## How long do press-on nails last?

There is no single number, because wear depends on how well you prepare your nails, which adhesive you use, and how hard you use your hands. Guides from nail brands give ranges, not promises. Many say adhesive tabs hold for a few days up to about a week, and nail glue holds for one to two weeks or more. Treat those as a rough guide.

Velvet Tips does not publish a fixed number of days for its sets. TODO: if the team has tested wear time on its own sets, add the figure and how it was tested here.

What you can control:

- Clean, dry, lightly buffed nails hold best.
- A set that fits wall to wall, without touching the skin, holds better than one that is too big or too small.
- Firm pressure for 20 to 30 seconds when you apply.
- Less time with your hands in hot water, especially in the first hour.

## Adhesive tabs: when to use them

Adhesive tabs are thin double-sided stickers that go between your nail and the press-on. Our how-to-apply guide recommends them for short wear.

Pick tabs when:

- You need the set for one event or a few days.
- You want to reuse the set again and again. Nothing hard is left on the back of the nail.
- You want the easiest removal.

Tabs may lift sooner if your hands are in water a lot or your nails are oily, so prep matters.

## Nail glue: when to use it

For longer wear, spread a thin layer of nail glue on the back of the press-on and a small amount on your natural nail. Press and hold firmly for 20 to 30 seconds.

Pick glue when:

- You want the set to last through a busy week or more.
- You use your hands a lot.
- You have short nails and want a stronger hold. (More on this in our guide to press-ons on short or bitten nails.)

Use a thin layer. Too much glue can ooze onto the skin and makes removal harder. Glue also leaves some residue on the back of the nail that you must clean before you reuse the set.

## How to make a set last longer

1. **Push back your cuticles** gently and buff the shine off each nail.
2. **Wipe every nail with the alcohol pad** from your kit to remove oil and lotion.
3. **Test the fit** before you stick anything. Each press-on should cover the nail wall to wall without touching the skin. Between two sizes? Choose the smaller one and file the edges. See our size guide.
4. **Press at a slight angle** at the cuticle, lower the nail flat and hold for 20 to 30 seconds. Start with your thumb and work outward.
5. **Go easy for the first hour.** Avoid long soaks and hot water.
6. **Avoid using your nails as tools**, such as to open cans or pick at labels.

## How to take them off, whichever you used

1. Soak your hands in warm soapy water for 10 to 15 minutes.
2. Lift each press-on gently from the side with a cuticle stick. Never pull it straight off.
3. Wipe off the leftover adhesive with an alcohol pad.
4. Store the set in its case.

If a nail will not budge, soak a little longer. Do not force it. Pulling is what damages natural nails.

## Reusing your set

Yes, press-ons can be worn again if you remove them gently and clean the adhesive off. Store them in the case they came in. Between sets, give your own nails a rest. See our guide to nail care between sets.

## FAQ

### Should I use glue or tabs for press-on nails?
Use tabs for short wear and easy removal. Use nail glue for longer wear. Glue holds longer than tabs, but it takes more care to remove. Every Velvet Tips set includes both adhesive tabs and nail glue.

### How long do press-on nails last?
It depends on prep, adhesive and daily hand use. Brand guides usually put tabs at a few days to about a week and glue at one to two weeks or more. Velvet Tips does not publish a fixed number of days.

### Can I use glue and tabs together?
Some press-on guides suggest combining them for extra hold. Velvet Tips guidance is to choose one: tabs for short wear, glue for longer wear. TODO: confirm with the team whether to recommend combining.

### Why do my press-on nails keep popping off?
The usual causes are oily or unprepared nails, a poor fit, too little pressure when applying, or too much water in the first hour. Re-prep with the alcohol pad, check the size, and press for 20 to 30 seconds.

### Does nail glue damage my natural nails?
Not when you apply and remove the set carefully. Damage mostly comes from pulling a set off. Soak your hands, lift from the side and rest your nails between sets.

### How do I remove press-ons that were put on with glue?
Soak your hands in warm soapy water for 10 to 15 minutes, lift each nail gently from the side with a cuticle stick, then clean off the leftover glue with an alcohol pad.

### Can I reuse press-on nails?
Yes. Remove them gently, clean off the adhesive and keep them in their case. Tabs are easiest to reuse with, because glue leaves residue that has to be cleaned first.

A file or buffer, adhesive tabs, nail glue, a cuticle pusher and an alcohol prep pad.
