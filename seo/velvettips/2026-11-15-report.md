**Not checked:** raw HTML (the fetch tool shows rendered text, so meta tags and JSON-LD may exist without being visible), live Google "People also ask" boxes, rankings, Search Console data, and the Instagram, Facebook and YouTube pages themselves (they need a login or render client-side). **Gap chosen:** "nail glue vs adhesive tabs". This is a high-intent comparison query. At least two competitors have dedicated pages, and the 365nails guide covers it as a full section. Velvet Tips ships both adhesives in every kit, but it answers the question in only one line on /faq. The site's own facts fully support the piece, so no numbers had to be invented. Piece: `2026-11-15-nail-glue-vs-adhesive-tabs.md`.
