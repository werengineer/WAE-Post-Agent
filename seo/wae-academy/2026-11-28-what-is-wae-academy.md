# What is WAE Academy?

## The short answer

WAE Academy is the learning arm of We Are Engineer (WAE). It runs practical, project-based tech courses in India. The programs listed on its website are Data Science & Analytics, Full Stack Development, AI & Machine Learning, and DevOps Engineering. Its Delhi centre is in Dwarka. The website lists typical course lengths of 3 to 6 months, with full-time and part-time options, and a certificate on completion.

## Who runs WAE Academy

WAE Academy is part of We Are Engineer, an engineering ecosystem that helps engineers learn, connect and get hired. ## Seven questions to ask before you join any tech course

These apply to WAE Academy and to every other institute. Ask them in writing and keep the answers.

1. **What will I build?** Ask for a list of projects, and look at one finished example.
2. **Who will teach my batch?** Ask for the trainer's name and work background.
3. **What exactly is in the syllabus?** Compare it with a recent job post for the role you want. Outdated tools are a warning sign.
4. **What does "placement support" or "internship" mean in writing?** Ask whether it is a promise or help with interviews, whether the internship is paid, and whether it is included in the fee.
5. **What if I do not get a job or internship?** A clear answer is a good sign.
6. **What is the total cost, and what is the refund rule?** Ask for the full fee, taxes and payment schedule on paper.
7. **Can I attend a demo class and speak to past students?** Do both if you can.
