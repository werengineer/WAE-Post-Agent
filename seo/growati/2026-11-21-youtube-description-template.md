# How to write a YouTube video description

## In short

- A YouTube description can be up to 5,000 characters. [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12948449?hl=en)
- Viewers see only the first few lines before they click to expand. Use those lines to describe the video. [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12948449?hl=en)
- Put channel information in the second part, the part viewers see after expanding. [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12948449?hl=en)
- Give every video its own description. Duplicates make a video harder to tell apart in search. [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12948449?hl=en)
- You do not need to use all 5,000 characters.

## What YouTube says a description is for

YouTube's help page on description tips gives a short list of advice. In plain words:

1. Describe the video in the first few lines.
2. Pick one or two main words that describe the video. Use them in the title and in the description.
3. Look for keywords and synonyms in the Research tab in YouTube Analytics and in Google Ads Keyword Planner.
4. Open the "How viewers find your video" reports in YouTube Analytics. If viewers use search terms your description does not contain, add them.
5. Add chapters, playlist links, credits and channel links.
6. Use a default description for the channel information that repeats on every upload.

Source: [Tips for video descriptions (YouTube Help)](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12948449?hl=en)

## How long can a YouTube description be?

The maximum is 5,000 characters. The visible part before "Show more" is much shorter, and how much shows depends on the device and where the video appears. YouTube does not give one number on that page, so write the first two lines as if they are all that many people will read.

## What to put in the first few lines

The first lines are the part viewers see before they click to expand. Write them for a person who has not decided to watch yet:

- Say what the video is about in plain words.
- Use the one or two main words you also used in the title.
- Do not start with a link list or a "subscribe" line. Put those lower down.

## A simple description template

Copy this and fill in the brackets.

```
[Line 1-2: What this video shows, in plain words. Include your main phrase once.]

In this video:
- [Point 1]
- [Point 2]
- [Point 3]

00:00 [Chapter title]
[mm:ss] [Chapter title]
[mm:ss] [Chapter title]

Watch next: [link to a related video or playlist]

Credits: [people and channels who helped]

About this channel:
[Default channel text: who you are, what you publish, how often.]
[Links you want people to find.]
```

YouTube's help page says bullet lists work if a line starts with `*`, `+` or `-` followed by a space.

## What to do with chapters and links

- Chapters go in the description. YouTube's rules: the first timestamp must be `00:00`, you need at least three timestamps, they must be in ascending order, and each chapter must be at least 10 seconds long. Source: [Video Chapters (YouTube Help)](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579?hl=en)
- If your chapters do not show, see [YouTube chapters not showing? Here's why](https://growati.com/blogs/youtube-chapters-not-showing).
- Link to related playlists. YouTube lists this as a tip.
- Credit collaborators and mention the channels and people who helped.

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Copying the same description onto every video.** YouTube advises a unique description for each video.
- **Burying the summary.** If the first lines are only links, the viewer learns nothing about the video.
- **Repeating the title with no new information.** Add what the video covers.
- **Writing keywords you do not use in the video.** YouTube says titles must be accurate; a mismatch can make viewers leave. Source: [Thumbnail & title tips (YouTube Help)](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12340300?hl=en)
- **Never updating old videos.** Search terms change. Check the "How viewers find your video" report and add missing terms.

## How to check your description after you publish

YouTube suggests checking how the description looks in search, on the watch page, and on several mobile devices. Do this for your first few videos, then keep the layout that works.

## FAQ

### How long can a YouTube description be?
Up to 5,000 characters, according to YouTube Help.

### How many characters of a YouTube description are visible?
YouTube does not publish one number. Viewers see the first few lines before they expand the text, and the amount varies by device. Put the summary first.

### What should I put in a YouTube description?
A plain-words summary of the video first. Then chapters for longer videos, a link to a related video or playlist, credits, and your channel information.

### Should every video have a different description?
Yes. YouTube says a unique description for each video helps it stand out in search.

### Where do I find keywords for my description?
YouTube suggests the Research tab in YouTube Analytics, Google Ads Keyword Planner, and the "How viewers find your video" reports.

### Can I use bullet points in a YouTube description?
Yes. Start the line with `*`, `+` or `-` followed by a space.

### Do chapters go in the description?
Yes. List timestamps in the description. The first must be 00:00, with at least three in ascending order, each chapter at least 10 seconds long.
