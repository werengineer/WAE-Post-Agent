# Why do my unlisted YouTube videos get no views?

## In short

Unlisted videos are not meant to get views from strangers. YouTube's help page says an Unlisted video can be seen by anyone with the link, but it does not appear in your channel's Videos tab or in YouTube search (unless someone adds it to a public playlist). If you want people to find a video, change its visibility to Public in YouTube Studio. Then fix the title, description and thumbnail, because an untitled video will still be hard to find.

## What Unlisted, Public and Private mean on YouTube

Based on YouTube's "Change video privacy settings" help page:

| Setting | Who can see it | Search and channel page |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Anyone on YouTube | Can appear in search results and related videos. Posted on your channel and shown in the subscriber feed. |
| Unlisted | Anyone with the link. No Google account needed. | Not on your channel's Videos tab. Not in search, unless someone adds it to a public playlist. |
| Private | Only you and people you choose | Not on your channel's Videos tab. Not in search. |

So an Unlisted video is a "link-only" video. It can get views, but only from people you send the link to.

## Why unlisted videos get almost no views

1. **YouTube search cannot show them.** Unlisted videos are left out of search results.
2. **They are not on your channel page.** A visitor browsing your Videos tab will not see them.
3. **Packaging is often missing.** Many unlisted videos are recordings uploaded fast, with a default file name as the title, no description and an auto-picked frame as the thumbnail. YouTube Help says search results depend on how well your title, description and content match the search, so a video with no real title gives search nothing to match.

Once a video is public, views are not guaranteed. YouTube's performance FAQ says YouTube does not promote videos to your audience but finds videos for viewers when they visit, and that subscribers are not the same as viewers.

## How to make one video public

From YouTube's help page:

1. Sign in to YouTube Studio.
2. In the left menu, select **Content**.
3. Point to the video.
4. Click the down arrow under **Visibility** and choose **Public**.
5. Select **Save**.

Before you press Save, check the title, description and thumbnail. You only get one "first public" moment per video (see the next section).

YouTube's performance FAQ also says that switching an unlisted video to public will not hurt its performance: "what matters is how viewers respond after it's been published."

## Old unlisted videos that turned Private

If a video was Unlisted for years and the link stopped working, check its visibility. YouTube's "Older unlisted content" page says that from July 23, 2021, Unlisted videos uploaded before January 1, 2017 were made Private unless the owner opted out. Viewers with the link can no longer watch them, and embedded copies stop working.

Your options, per that page:

- **Keep it Private:** do nothing. Share with up to 50 email addresses if needed.
- **Keep it Unlisted with the same URL:** switch it back to "legacy" Unlisted. Old links and embeds work, but the 2017 security update does not apply.
- **Make it Public:** anyone can see it and it may appear in search and recommendations. Views and comments are kept.
- **Re-upload as a new Unlisted video:** new URL, views and comments do not carry over.

## What to fix before you publish a big backlog

Making a video public only makes it findable. To be found for the right searches, each video needs:

- **A title** that says what the video is about, in the words a viewer would search.
- **A description** that explains the video and, if useful, links to related videos.
- **A thumbnail** that shows the topic. YouTube Help warns that misleading titles or thumbnails can turn away potential viewers, so keep them honest.
- **Chapters,** where the video is long.

If you have a handful of videos, do this by hand in Studio. If you have hundreds, it is a batch job, and it takes time.

## FAQ

### Why do my unlisted YouTube videos have no views?
Unlisted videos do not appear in YouTube search or on your channel's Videos tab. Only people who have the link can watch. If you want strangers to find a video, make it Public in YouTube Studio and give it a clear title, description and thumbnail.

### Do unlisted videos show up in search?
No. YouTube's help page says Unlisted videos will not appear in search unless someone adds the video to a public playlist. Public videos can appear in search results and related videos.

### How do I change an unlisted video to public?
In YouTube Studio, open Content, point to the video, click the down arrow under Visibility, choose Public, and select Save.

### Will making an unlisted video public hurt its performance?
No. YouTube's performance FAQ says switching an unlisted video to public will not hurt performance, and that what matters is how viewers respond after it is published.

### Are subscribers notified when I make an unlisted video public?
YouTube's "Older unlisted content" page says subscribers are notified the first time content goes public. TODO: confirm current behavior in YouTube Studio, including for bulk changes.

### Why did my old unlisted video stop working?
Unlisted videos uploaded before January 1, 2017 were made Private from July 23, 2021, unless the owner opted out. You can keep them Private, switch them back to legacy Unlisted, make them Public, or re-upload.

### Can unlisted videos be embedded on a website?
Yes. Unlisted videos can be watched by anyone with the link, which includes embeds. The exception is old videos made Private in 2021: YouTube says embedded copies of those stopped working.
