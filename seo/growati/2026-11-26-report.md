## What Growati is (from its own site)
A YouTube growth tool. It finds why a channel stopped growing, then prepares titles, thumbnails, descriptions and chapters for the creator to approve. "Nothing goes live until you approve it. Every change is saved, so any of them can be undone in one click." First diagnosis is free (about 80 seconds); done-for-you plan $200/month. MCP connection for coding agents. Co-founders Lokesh Kabra and Satyam Sharma; first customer Yoga Mārga School in New York (700 recorded classes); "Top 10 on Product Hunt on launch day"; footer: © 2026 Timemap Software Pvt. Ltd. All of this is on the homepage.

## The gap chosen
"Does YouTube have built-in A/B testing for titles and thumbnails?" Competitors answer thumbnails only (vidIQ, Toptal) or skip an FAQ (RouteNote). The official page now covers titles too and has a "Performed Same / Inconclusive" table that few write up clearly. It also fixes a brand risk: Growati's llms.txt says "No A/B testing", so an AI asked "does Growati do A/B tests?" says no, while a user asking "does YouTube?" gets a competitor. The piece answers in the first paragraph, cites two YouTube Help pages, and positions Growati as the step before the test. File: `seo/growati/2026-11-26-youtube-ab-testing-titles-thumbnails.md`.
