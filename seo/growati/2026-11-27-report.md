A YouTube growth tool. It finds why a channel or its videos stopped growing, then prepares titles, thumbnails, descriptions, tags and chapters that the creator approves. "Nothing goes live until you approve it." Version history with one-click restore. First diagnosis free (about 80 seconds); done-for-you plan $200/month. MCP connection for Claude Code, Cursor, Claude and ChatGPT. First customer Yoga Mārga School (700 classes). Blog bylines use "Satyam Sharma, Co-founder and CTO".
