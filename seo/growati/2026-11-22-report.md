# Growati SEO + AEO + GEO report, 2026-11-22

## What Growati is (from its own site)
A YouTube channel growth service. Per the homepage: it finds why a channel stopped growing, then fixes titles, thumbnails, descriptions, tags and chapters video by video, with approval before anything goes live. Free channel check (public numbers, no sign-in), diagnosis of the last 30 videos, one-click restore of saved originals, MCP connection for Claude Code, Cursor, Claude and ChatGPT, and a $200/month done-for-you plan. Footer: "© 2026 Timemap Software Pvt. Ltd."; co-founders Lokesh Kabra and Satyam Sharma. First customer named on the site: Yoga Mārga School (New York).
