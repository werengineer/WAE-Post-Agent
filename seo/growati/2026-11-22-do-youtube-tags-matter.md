## In short

- YouTube's own help page says tags "play a minimal role" in discovery.
- Tags help most when your topic is commonly misspelled.
- Your title, thumbnail and description matter more, so fix those first.
- Do not stuff tags. YouTube says excessive tags in a video description go against its spam policy.
- Spend about two minutes per video on tags. No more.

## What YouTube says about tags

YouTube's help page on tags is short. It says tags "play a minimal role in your video's discovery." It also says your video's title, thumbnail, and description are "more important pieces of metadata."

The one use it names is spelling. Tags "can be useful if the content of your video is commonly misspelled." If viewers often type your topic wrong, a tag with the wrong spelling can help them find you.

Source: [YouTube Help: Add tags to videos](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/146402?hl=en)

## When tags still help

Tags are a small signal. They are worth a few words in these cases:

- **Misspellings.** Your topic, product or name is often spelled wrong.
- **Alternate names.** People call the same thing two ways. Put the second name in a tag.
- **Your brand or channel name.** One tag so the video is tied to you.
- **Topic you cannot say in the title.** A short title may leave out a related phrase. A tag can carry it.

If none of these apply, a few plain tags are fine. Do not expect them to change your views.

## What matters more than tags

YouTube points to three fields first:

1. **Title.** Say what the video is, in the words a viewer would type.
2. **Thumbnail.** It decides whether the title gets clicked.
3. **Description.** The first lines are what viewers and search see first.

If a video is not getting views, check these before you touch tags. Our guide to [how to improve YouTube CTR](/blogs/how-to-improve-youtube-ctr) is a good next step, and [YouTube chapters not showing](/blogs/youtube-chapters-not-showing) covers description rules.

## How to fill in tags in two minutes

1. Open the video in YouTube Studio and go to **Details**, then **Show more**.
2. Add your main topic phrase as the first tag. Many guides recommend this. YouTube has not said the first tag counts more, so treat it as habit, not a rule.
3. Add one or two alternate names or common misspellings of your topic.
4. Add your channel or brand name.
5. Stop. Five to eight short tags is plenty. Third-party guides differ on the number, and YouTube gives no target. (Ahrefs notes that the total across all tags is capped at 500 characters. TODO: confirm the current cap in YouTube Studio before publishing.)

## Common tag mistakes

- **Stuffing.** Dozens of tags will not help, and YouTube says excessive tags in the description are against its spam policy.
- **Misleading tags.** Do not tag a topic the video is not about.
- **Tags in the description.** Tags belong in the tags field, not pasted into the description.
- **Copying a competitor's whole list.** Their video is not yours. Use only tags that fit your video.
- **Spending an hour on tags.** The time is better spent on the title and thumbnail.

## FAQ

### Do YouTube tags still matter?
A little. YouTube says tags "play a minimal role" in discovery. They can help when your topic is commonly misspelled. Title, thumbnail and description matter more, so fix those first.

### How many tags should I use on YouTube?
YouTube gives no target number. Many guides suggest about five to eight short, relevant tags. Quality matters more than count. Stuffing tags can break YouTube's spam policy.

### What is the best first tag?
Many creators use their main topic phrase as the first tag. YouTube has not confirmed that the first tag counts more. It is a common habit, not a proven rule.

### Are tags the same as hashtags?
No. Tags sit in the hidden tags field in YouTube Studio. Hashtags are words with a # that appear in the title or description and are visible to viewers.

### Can bad tags hurt my channel?
Misleading tags or excessive tags can go against YouTube's spam, deceptive practices and scams policy. Use only tags that match your video.

### Should I add tags to old videos?
Only if it is quick. If an old video has no tags and its topic is often misspelled, add a few. Fix the title, thumbnail and description first.
