# Do YouTube tags still matter?

## The short answer

Barely. YouTube's own Help page says: "Your video's title, thumbnail, and description are more important pieces of metadata for your video's discovery." It adds that "tags play a minimal role in your video's discovery." Tags are still worth a few minutes per video, mainly for words people often misspell. They will not rescue a video with a weak title or thumbnail.

## What YouTube itself says about tags

YouTube describes tags as "descriptive keywords you can add to your video to help viewers find your content." The same page makes three points that matter:

- Title, thumbnail and description matter more than tags for discovery.
- Tags can be useful "if the content of your video is commonly misspelled."
- Adding excessive tags to your video description is against YouTube's policies on spam, deceptive practices and scams.

Outside tests agree. vidIQ ran a small test in 2020 using a made-up search term. As a tag, it did not make the video findable. As part of the title, the video became searchable in about 10 minutes. vidIQ says the test was small, with one search term. Ahrefs reports that studies show only a small positive link between keywords in tags and ranking, and that only about a third of top-ranking videos use the keyword in their tags. That is correlation, not proof that tags cause rankings.

## When tags still help

Tags are a small tool for a few cases:

1. **Commonly misspelled names or words.** This is the case YouTube names itself.
2. **Technical terms or jargon** that automatic captions may get wrong. Yoast makes this point, along with weak captions in some non-English videos. This is Yoast's reading, not a YouTube statement.
3. **Alternate names for the same thing**, such as a nickname and a full name, when both are real search terms.

If none of these fit your video, a short list of plain tags is enough.

## Tags vs hashtags

They are different things. Tags sit in the video's details in YouTube Studio and viewers do not see them. Hashtags are written in the title or description and show up as links. Yoast notes that the first three hashtags appear above the video title, and suggests three to five in the description. Do not paste your tag list into the description: YouTube's policy treats excessive tags there as spam.

## How many tags should you use?

YouTube does not give a target number. Ahrefs says the field allows a total of 500 characters. Yoast says there is "no right or wrong number" and suggests five to twenty feels about right. A good rule: stop when you run out of relevant terms. Never add a tag that does not describe the video.

## A simple way to fill in tags in five minutes

1. Write the main phrase a viewer would type to find the video. Make it the first tag.
2. Add the name of the person, product or place in the video, in the spelling you use.
3. Add one or two common misspellings or alternate names, only if they are real.
4. Add two or three plain phrases that describe the topic.
5. Stop. Do not copy tags from other channels' videos that do not describe yours.

## What to fix before you touch tags

If a video is not getting views, the cause is rarely the tag box. Check these first, in this order:

1. **The title:** does it say clearly what the video gives the viewer?
2. **The thumbnail:** does it match the title and stand out at small size?
3. **The first lines of the description:** do they state the topic in plain words?
4. **Chapters:** do they help viewers jump to what they came for?

## Frequently asked questions

### Do YouTube tags still matter in 2026?
Only a little. YouTube says tags play a minimal role in discovery, and that title, thumbnail and description matter more. They help most when your video's content is commonly misspelled.

### How many tags should I use on YouTube?
YouTube gives no number. The tag field allows 500 characters in total, according to Ahrefs. Yoast suggests five to twenty. Use only tags that describe the video.

### Do tags help a video rank in YouTube search?
Studies show at most a small link. Ahrefs reports only about a third of top-ranking videos use the keyword in their tags. vidIQ's small test found a title keyword made a video searchable faster than a tag did.

### What is the difference between tags and hashtags on YouTube?
Tags are hidden keywords in the video details. Hashtags are typed in the title or description and are visible links. The first three hashtags appear above the video title, according to Yoast.

### Can too many tags hurt my video?
YouTube says adding excessive tags to your video description is against its spam policy. Keep tags relevant and short, and keep them out of the description.
